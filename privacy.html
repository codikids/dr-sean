Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

What We Collect

When you send a direct message to our Instagram account, we process your message content to provide automated responses. We also store your Instagram user ID and message history temporarily to maintain conversation context.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide automated DM responses and improve our service. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

Data Retention

Conversation history is automatically deleted after 7 days. DM logs are retained for up to 30 days and then permanently deleted.

Data Security

All data is stored securely using Cloudflare's infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us via Instagram DM.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please reach out via Instagram DM.
